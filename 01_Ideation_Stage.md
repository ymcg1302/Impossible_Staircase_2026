## Ideation and Initial Pipeline

My initial idea was to make an Alice and Wonderland themed Escher-Staircase project according to the brief stated [here.](https://brightspace.bournemouth.ac.uk/d2l/le/lessons/675125/topics/2499691) I based was planning to create a castle structure based on some of Escher's most famous works: 
* [The House of Stairs](https://uploads5.wikiart.org/images/m-c-escher/house-of-stairs.jpg)
* [Relativity](http://www.scottmcd.net/artanalysis/?p=548)
* [Belvedere](https://www.artchive.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/belvedere-mc-escher-1958.jpg)
* [Waterfall](https://ih1.redbubble.net/image.3879990539.3391/flat,750x,075,f-pad,750x1000,f8f8f8.jpg)
* and lastly, and probably his most famous [Ascending and Descending](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascending_and_Descending#/media/File:Ascending_and_Descending.jpg) as a direct example of how to construct the Impossible Staircase. 

With the following procedural elements:
* A Python Algorithm in Maya to construct [a House of Cards]
* [Revolved Chess Pieces](https://share.google/aimode/RRODhiQHgapbiHlTZ) for the supports and columns of the card castle
* And lastly, [Tiled Texture Elements] from Substance Painter to create the Cards' Patterns.

However, upon further reflection and after being showed several examples in class, I decided to simplify the concept to balance it with my other projects, to maintain scope and achievability and lastly, to bring the focus of the project back on the bouncing ball and _not_ the architecture itself. I found that the new idea (shown below) supports the ball bouncing animation much better, keeps the output and creation of the environment much simpler, and the overall idea much tighter and cleaner. Further feedback still needs to be sought but my new proposal includes the following: 

* Focusing on the Impossible Staircase itself by making the outcome a Chess Board inspired by this artist: [Sandro] who was directly inspired by M.C. Escher as well to explore optical illusions through complex designs. This also keeps the structure of the staircase the main focus, allowing me to remove superfluous design elements and unnecessary embellishment.
* The texture itself is inspired by M.C. Escher's lithograph designs by using an aiToonShader in Autodesk Maya as seen [here] and [here], allowing me to design interesting lighting scenarios that support the dynamic interactions between the objects as seen in the inspirations I gathered below.
* Lastly, this allows me to play around with other potential procedural elements such as using PyMel to place the objects in scene and help with the initial animation testing of the ball's path. 

I am now more eager to start and see where this project will take me and will continue to document my progress as I move forward.
